# Data audit: CaryxAI Everyday Manipulation `EM1-0406`

**Decision:** NO-GO as the primary `place_demo`; GO for the nonsymmetric-object
rotation ablation and retained as a reflection stress test.

## What the sequence shows

- Dataset source: CaryxAI Everyday Manipulation 3D, raw clip `EM1-0406`.
- Duration and cadence: 13.11 seconds at 29.908 FPS (392 RGB frames).
- Visual action: one hand picks up a black action camera from the table, places it
  onto a square white plate, then withdraws.
- Capture: portrait 1440 × 1920 RGB-D from an iPhone/ARKit recording. The raw
  archive includes RGB, depth, confidence, intrinsics, and camera poses.

observable, which is more useful for reconstruction and pose-conditioned policy
learning than the bottle's yaw-symmetric shape. The sequence still contains a
glass tabletop and strong reflections; mask the table/reflection region during
reconstruction and validate the recovered camera trajectory.

## Rotation ablation outcome

The initial action-camera pose in frame 0 and 13 fully visible placed poses from
frames 330–390 support a controlled image-plane experiment. A SIFT/RANSAC similarity
fit uses two thirds of geometrically verified correspondences and evaluates on every
third correspondence after spatial sorting. Relative to mask centroid, area, and
principal-axis fitting, tracked rotation reduces median held-out error from 34.89 px
to 0.70 px over 82 points. The raw archive SHA-256, per-frame counts, confidence
intervals, fixed ROIs, and limitations are recorded in
`docs/experiments/em1-0406-rotation-ablation.json`.

## Reconstruction proportion outcome

Frame 0 also provides 609 aligned depth samples under the audited object mask. A
robust PCA box over the visible surfaces measures 81.9 × 60.6 × 35.4 mm. TripoSR-128
and Stable Fast 3D were run on the same larger asymmetric crop and aligned only by
their longest extent. TripoSR underestimates thickness by 58.9%; Stable Fast 3D by
33.1%. Neither satisfies the frozen 15% secondary-extent gate. Metric RGB-D or fitted
primitives remain required for geometry, while TripoSR remains the smaller watertight
visual proposal. The full evidence is in
`docs/experiments/em1-0406-reconstruction-proportions.json`.
