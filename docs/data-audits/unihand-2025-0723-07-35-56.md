# Data audit: UniHand `2025-0723-07-35-56`

**Decision:** GO — current `place_demo` for the fixed-camera RGB-D baseline.

## Sequence

- Action: a hand transfers a rigid white cup onto a square serving tray.
- Surface: a textured wood tabletop with diffuse illumination. Visual inspection
  shows no glass layer or mirror-like reflection.
- Capture: 35 synchronized RGB and depth frames from a RealSense L515 RGB-D camera.
- Native cadence: 30 FPS assumed from the dataset capture convention, yielding a
  roughly 1.17-second clip. Contact starts at frame 18; terminal contact is frame 34.

## Why it is the best current input

The rigid, high-contrast cup and visually distinct tray are easy to prompt and
track. Most importantly, the matte textured surface removes the reflection failure
mode that rejected the CaryxAI candidates. The sequence also supplies per-frame
depth and a point cloud.

## Limits and handling

- The camera is fixed and the clip is short. It is therefore unsuitable for a
  moving-camera/3DGS reconstruction claim.
- Use it for segmentation, point tracking, depth lifting, contact timing, and a
  fixed-camera RGB-D place baseline.
- Preserve all 35 frames during intake (`sample_fps: 30`); do not downsample to 10
  FPS.
